ABOUT THE PROJECT

Security that
feels simple.

Cyber Guardian turns everyday online safety checks into a quick, understandable workflow.

THE IDEA

Awareness before action

Phishing, weak passwords and scam messages often look ordinary. This project helps you pause, inspect the warning signs and make a more informed decision.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Private by default

Scan history stays in your browser. The dashboard is designed for quick checks without building a profile of your activity.
